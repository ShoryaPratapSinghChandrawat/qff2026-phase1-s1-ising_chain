### AI Assistance

**The AI's Role (The 20%):**
AI tools assisted the team strictly with supportive and analytical tasks. This included reviewing project files, suggesting code and documentation improvements, developing automated tests, and helping to interpret simulation and test output.

**The Team's Role (The 80%):**
The team provided the core 80% of the project's substantive value and architectural logic. The team is fully responsible for directing and reviewing the implementation, manually verifying all reported results, and deeply understanding the submitted work. All numerical results are based directly on the project's implemented calculations and saved outputs, ensuring complete human oversight, scientific integrity, and accountability.
